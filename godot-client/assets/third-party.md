# District and HUD assets

- [Medieval Village MegaKit Standard by Quaternius](https://store.godotengine.org/asset/quaternius/medieval-village-megakit/), CC0 1.0. The detailed district uses `Wall_Plaster_Straight`, `Window_Wide_Flat1`, `Roof_RoundTiles_8x8` and `Roof_Front_Brick8`. The glTF meshes and UVs are from the original. Color maps were resized to 1024 px and normal maps to 512 px. Color maps use lossy import compression. Materials use mipmapped textures, reduced normal strength, constant roughness and opaque glass for the exterior-only buildings.
- [Mechanized Magic by Dumivid](https://store.godotengine.org/asset/dumivid/mechanized-magic-ultimate-ui-pack/), CC0 1.0. The HUD uses icons 2, 24 and 19 from the small flat-gray PNG set.
